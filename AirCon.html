Single split

  • One indoor head, one outdoor unit
  • Best for a living room, main bedroom or a single open space
  • Cheapest way to cool the room you actually use
  • Quick install, usually half a day

Multi-split

  • Several indoor heads on one outdoor unit
  • Each room controlled separately
  • Only one outdoor unit taking up space outside
  • Suits homes where ducted isn't practical

Sizing by room

A rough guide. Ceiling height, insulation, window area and which way the room faces all shift it, which is why we measure on site.

Bedroom2.5 kWUp to about 20 m²
Bedroom / study3.5 kWAbout 20–30 m²
Living room5.0 kWAbout 30–45 m²
Open plan7.1 kW+45 m² and above

Oversizing is a common mistake — a unit that's too big short-cycles, controls humidity poorly and uses more power than a right-sized one.

VEU rebate on air conditioning

  • Victorian Energy Upgrades can discount eligible replacements
  • Usually applies when an old or inefficient system is being replaced
  • The discount comes off the quote — you don't claim it back later
  • We check eligibility for your property before you commit

Running it on solar

  • A split system draws roughly 1–2 kW while working hard
  • On a hot afternoon a 6.6 kW solar system covers that easily
  • The cost shows up on summer evenings and winter mornings
  • That evening window is exactly what a battery covers
