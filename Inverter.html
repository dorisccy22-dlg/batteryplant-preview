What the inverter decides

ConversionDC → ACHow much of what your roof makes actually reaches your power points.
Battery controlcharge & dischargeWhen to store, when to release, and when to buy from the grid.
Backupon or offWhether chosen circuits keep running when the street loses power.

Hybrid or string

  • A hybrid inverter handles panels and battery in one unit
  • A string inverter only handles panels — a battery needs its own
  • If a battery is coming later, a hybrid now saves money then
  • Everything on this page is hybrid-capable

Sizing it right

  • Inverter size is usually a little under the panel array size
  • Oversizing the array slightly is normal and allowed
  • Leave headroom if you plan to add panels or storage
  • Three phase homes need a three phase inverter

Common questions

Solar panels and batteries produce DC power; your house runs on AC. The inverter converts between the two, and on a hybrid unit it also decides when the battery charges, when it discharges, and when power comes from the grid.

If you have a battery now, or want one later, a hybrid handles both panels and battery in one unit. A string inverter only handles panels, so adding storage later means adding a second inverter. We quote hybrid by default unless a battery is clearly off the table.
