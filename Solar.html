Common questions

A 6.6 kW system is roughly 15 panels, 10 kW around 22, and 13.2 kW around 30 — the exact count depends on panel wattage and the usable roof area once you allow for vents, valleys and shading.

It cuts the part of the bill you use while the sun is up. If the house is empty during the day, most of the solar is exported for a small credit and bought back at peak rates later — that's the gap a battery closes.

Small-scale certificates are applied as a discount at the point of sale, and Victorian programs may apply on top. Amounts and eligibility change each year, so we confirm what applies to your property in writing.

Most rooftop systems are a one day job. Solar plus battery usually takes one to two days. Grid approval before and after the install is handled by us.

Melbourne rain does most of the work. We recommend a visual check every few years, and the monitoring app will show you if output drops off unexpectedly.
